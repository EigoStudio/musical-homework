Winter — The Mystery of the Two Snows

1. See You Next Spring!

Winter arrives in the small town. The Ants are singing as they march back to their cozy homes to sleep until spring.

2. Snowman Contest

Plum and Fortune are working hard to build a giant snowman that is bigger than a sumo wrestler.

3. I Want the Beautiful Gown!

Everyone in the town is competing in the "Snowman Contest". The person who makes the last standing snowman will win a beautiful gown as a prize. Snow (who is actually an Imposter in disguise) appears and watches them. When Snow says, "I must win that gown," Luna laughs and says, "Don't be silly. I've never seen you work hard." Snow smiles mysteriously and says, "Who said anything about hard work?"

4. A Suspicious Afternoon at the Store

It is a bright afternoon. The Imposter goes to Bravey’s store with a dark plan. She is looking for sugar to build a fake snowman. She finds a large jar and confirms it is sugar. Then, to make sure everyone thinks the real Snow is a thief, she does something very strange. In an unnaturally loud voice, she shouts, "Oh, there it is! A big jar of sugar!". She wants the neighbors to hear her. She grabs the jar and runs off.

5. The Honest Mochi

Meanwhile, Bravey is enjoying tea with her friends Melody, Tiny, and Zoe. Bravey shows them her new product Honest Mochi. "If you are an honest person, it turns pink," Bravey explains. "But if you are a liar, it turns gray!". Tiny and Zoe look nervous. But Melody is brave and takes a bite. The mochi turns bright pink! Next, Tiny takes a bite, and then Zoe does too. It turns pink for both of them! All four friends cheer together, "We are honest!".

6. Who took My Sugar?

After their tea time, Bravey returns to her kitchen and gasps. "My sugar! It's gone! Who took my sugar?" A neighbor calls out, "I saw Snow sneaking into your store! I even heard her shouting about sugar!".

7. Putting Snow to the Test

The girls find the real Snow, but she is in a huge hurry to perform her snow art at a festival in the city. "Did you take Bravey's sugar?" they ask. Snow looks completely confused. Bravey offers her a piece of Honest Mochi. As she hasn't eaten anything all day, Snow gladly accepts it. When she takes a bite, it turns pink! The girls are deeply puzzled. "She is honest..." they whisper.

8. Look at My Perfect Snowman

In the town square, many snowmen are lined up. The "Fake Snow" stands by a small snowman. She shouts, "Look at my snowman! It's small, but it's perfect! It will NEVER melt, and I will win!". The townspeople are shocked. "That's impossible!". But the Fake Snow just smirks and says, "Not if you use... a secret ingredient".

9. The Ogre’s Grudge (Flashback)

Years ago, the Ogre ate 200 of Snow’s favorite dried persimmons. When Snow caught her, she didn't apologize. To teach her a lesson, Snow trapped her in ice for three long days. It was a terrible experience. She was freezing and shivering. She felt so humiliated that she decided to get revenge by pretending to be Snow and making everyone think she is a thief.

10. Something's not Right

The girls are looking at the Fake Snow suspiciously. They have just seen the real Snow leave the town a few minutes ago. Tiny feels something is wrong. She uses her magic mallet to shrink and slips into the pocket of this strange person to investigate.

11. Sugar Snowman

Ants wake up because of the sweet smell of sugar. They march and carry the sugar back to their nest. Very quickly, the "perfect" snowman begins to crumble! The Fake Snow starts crying, "Oh no! I used Bravey's sugar!". Tiny pops out and shouts, "You are not Snow! Who are you?". The Fake Snow screams in a scary Ogre voice, "I AM SNOW!".

12. Forgiveness and Soup

The real Snow arrives. The Ogre admits to trying to frame her. Snow explains her side and then says, "I forgive you". Bravey suggests red bean soup. "Yes, please!!" everyone shouts happily. They all enjoy the warm soup together.
